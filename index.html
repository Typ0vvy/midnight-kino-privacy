Midnight Kino: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Android game Midnight Kino (package com.Midnight_Kino, the "Game") uses, and why. The Game is published by Enigma Verse (developer: Marcin Kopeć), contact: enigma.verse@yahoo.com.

1. What we collect ourselves

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, e-mail address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. We run no servers for this Game that receive your data.

2. What stays on your device

The Game stores your settings (language, volume, graphics, controls), your best results (highest wave, kills, time) and which hints you have already seen, in the local storage of your device. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data.

3. Advertising

The Game is free and shows ads. After a defeat you may see a full-screen ad, and you can choose to watch an optional ad to revive once per run. Ads are served by a third-party advertising provider (Google AdMob, Google LLC). To show and measure ads, that provider and its partners may collect and process information from your device, for example: your advertising ID, IP address, device model and operating system version, app version, language, and information about how you interact with ads. This is governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the Game asks for your consent for personalised advertising when you first start it (a consent form from the advertising provider). You can change your choice later by clearing the Game's data in your device settings or by reinstalling the Game, which shows the consent form again. Without consent, you may see non-personalised ads. You can also reset or turn off your advertising ID in your device's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

4. Children

The Game is intended for players aged 16 and over, and contains violence and blood. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If you believe a child has used the Game, contact us and we will help.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your data. The only information shared with third parties is what the advertising provider collects as described in section 3.

6. Your rights

If you are in the EEA, the UK or another region with data protection laws, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to withdraw consent at any time, and to complain to your data protection authority. Because we do not hold your personal data ourselves, requests about advertising data should be sent to the advertising provider (see section 3); you can also contact us at the address above and we will help you with them.

7. Security and retention

The data stored on your device stays there until you delete it. We take reasonable care to keep the Game free of unnecessary data collection.

8. Changes

We may update this policy, for example when a new version of the Game adds features. The date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be announced in the Game or on its store page.
